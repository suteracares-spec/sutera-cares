Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated 5 October 2026

Looking after someone means we learn personal things about them and their family. This notice explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It is written to meet Malaysia's Personal Data Protection Act 2010 (PDPA). A Bahasa Malaysia or Chinese copy is available on request.

Who we are

Sutera Care Provider supplies non-medical caregivers to homes in the Klang Valley. We are the data user responsible for the information described here. Our non-profit sister organisation, Sutera Cares, is a separate organisation and does not receive our clients' information.

What we collect

When you send an enquiry

  • Your name, phone number, email and relationship to the person needing care.
  • The name, age, area and mobility of the person needing care, and what help they need.
  • The date and time you sent the form and the internet address it came from, so we can spot abuse.

When care goes ahead

  • Contact and address details for the client and the family members you name.
  • Health and care information needed to write and follow the care plan — for example, conditions, allergies, mobility, routines and house rules. Under the PDPA this is sensitive personal data, and we only process it with your explicit consent.
  • Visit records: the scheduled times, check-in and check-out times, the caregiver's location at check-in, and the caregiver's visit notes.
  • Concerns raised by the family, the caregiver or the office, and how they were resolved.
  • Invoices and the payments you make by bank transfer. We do not collect or store card details.

When you apply to work with us

  • Your name, contact details, the area you can work in, languages, experience, availability and training.
  • If you are shortlisted: references, right-to-work documents and the outcome of a police record check, with your consent.
  • If you join us: your identity card number (stored encrypted), schedule, visit records and pay records.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and arrange a home assessment.
  • To write the care plan, match a caregiver, schedule visits and arrange cover.
  • To show the family what happened on each visit, and to deal with concerns.
  • To invoice and record payments, and to meet our legal and accounting duties.
  • To assess job applications and to vet caregivers before placement.

We do not sell personal information, use it for advertising, or send marketing messages unless you ask us to.

Who can see it

  • Your assigned caregiver sees the client's care plan and the details needed for each visit — and only for clients they are assigned to.
  • Your coordinator and our office staff see what they need to run the placement. Office accounts are protected with two-step sign-in, and access to records is logged.
  • Family members you name can see the schedule, visit notes and invoices in the family portal.
  • Our service providers, such as our web host, store data on our behalf under contract and may not use it for anything else.
  • Authorities, only where the law requires it, or where someone is at serious risk of harm.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that don't become a placement are deleted within 12 months. Unsuccessful job applications are deleted within 12 months unless you ask us to keep you on file. Client care records and financial records are kept for 7 years after care ends, to meet tax and legal obligations, and then deleted.

How we protect it

The portal is served only over an encrypted connection. Identity card numbers are encrypted in the database. Staff accounts need two-step sign-in, every sign-in requires a personal account, and access to client records is recorded in an audit log. Caregivers see only their own clients.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you may:

  • ask for a copy of the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to correct anything that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • withdraw consent, or ask us to limit how we use your information — though we may then be unable to continue providing care; and
  • tell us you do not want to receive any marketing.

Send your request to the email below. We reply within 21 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, and we will tell you if the law allows us to refuse.

Contact

Questions, requests or complaints about personal information: care@suteracares.org, with "Privacy request" in the subject line. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Personal Data Protection Department (Jabatan Perlindungan Data Peribadi).

If this notice changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
